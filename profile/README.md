# E³ Ecosystem

Practical homeowner education built around efficiency, energy, and economics.

E³ Ecosystem helps homeowners better understand their homes, identify practical opportunities, and make more informed decisions. The public starting point is the **free E³ Homeowner Checkup**.

## Current public work

- Free homeowner education and checkup tools
- Clear explanations of home efficiency, energy, and household economics
- Public documentation and carefully limited technical resources
- E3 Pulse, the foundational digital and rewards layer being developed within the broader ecosystem

## Projects

### E³ Homeowner Checkup

A free educational checkup designed to help homeowners organize what they observe and identify useful next steps.

### E3 Pulse

The foundational digital layer of E³ Ecosystem. Existing development history remains in the [E3 Pulse repository](https://github.com/e3-pulse/e3-pulse).
